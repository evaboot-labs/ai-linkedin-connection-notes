# Who you write to, and how

Status: EXAMPLE. Your agent asks you and replaces this line with "Status: yours" once the file is yours.

## Who

- **Roles:** Heads of Sales, VPs of Sales, sales and revenue directors.
- **Signal:** changed jobs in the last 90 days (Sales Navigator's "Changed jobs" filter).
- **Where:** United Kingdom. **Companies:** software, 51 to 500 employees.
- **How many a day:** 20. Raise it slowly, and only if your acceptance rate holds.

## You (never pitched in the note)

- **Name and role:** <your name>, <your role> at <your company>.
- **Why you connect:** you sell to sales teams and want to learn how new leaders set up their team.

## Rules for every note

1. **300 characters at most.** Shorter is better: 150 to 220.
2. **One real hook per note**, from the lead's export only: the new role, the company's growth, or one line of their own headline or summary. Name the hook.
3. **No pitch, no link, no product name, no meeting request.** A connection note opens a door; the conversation comes after the accept.
4. **No flattery beyond a short congratulation** on a new role.
5. **Vary the ending.** Do not end every note with the same phrase.
6. **Skip when there is nothing real to say.** Write the reason. A skipped lead is better than a generic note.
